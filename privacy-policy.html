Data Ethics & Confidentiality

Privacy Policy & Data Handling

Carl Rogers Counselling and Therapy Centre (CRCT) is committed to protecting your privacy and treating all personal and enquiry details with the highest standards of professional discretion.

Notice for Client Review:

This privacy document is provided as a transparent, professional template tailored to CRCT's operational model. Before final deployment, please have your legal counsel or management review and approve these clauses to ensure alignment with local data protection regulations.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or submit an appointment enquiry, we collect only the minimum necessary information required to respond to your request:

  • Contact details: Your full name, telephone / mobile number, and optional email address.
  • Enquiry context: The general category of support you are interested in (e.g., autism information, parent guidance, counselling, or workshops) and any voluntary message you choose to include.
  • Technical data: Non-personally identifiable server logs (such as browser type and timestamp) maintained standardly by web hosting servers for security and uptime maintenance.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected through this website is used exclusively for:

  • Responding to your specific questions or service enquiries.
  • Scheduling consultation appointments at our Visakhapatnam clinic.
  • Providing necessary logistical information regarding prior appointments and clinic directions.

3. Protection of Healthcare Data & Form Caution

Important Privacy Caution: General website forms and standard internet emails are not designed for transmitting highly sensitive medical, clinical, or diagnostic histories. We strongly advise visitors not to include detailed medical documentation, psychiatric medication histories, or confidential legal records through our online contact form.

Such confidential clinical records should be shared directly in-person with our licensed professionals during your scheduled consultation.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

CRCT operates on strict psychological ethics. We never sell, rent, trade, or distribute your contact details or enquiry information to commercial third parties, advertising networks, or marketing agencies.

Information may only be disclosed under exceptional legal circumstances where required by law or a valid court order, or to prevent immediate, serious physical harm to an individual.

5. Cookies & Website Analytics

Our static website is designed to be lightweight and respectful of your privacy. It does not use intrusive third-party tracking cookies or advertising tracking pixels. If basic hosting analytics are enabled by the web server (such as standard Hostinger access statistics), they collect aggregated, anonymous data that cannot identify you personally.

6. Data Retention & Rights

You have the right to enquire about any contact information we hold regarding your correspondence and request its correction or deletion. Enquiries that do not lead to an active clinical relationship are routinely and safely purged.

7. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your enquiry is processed, please contact us:
