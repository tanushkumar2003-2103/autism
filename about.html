Carl Rogers Counselling & Therapy Centre

Dedicated to Healing, Empathy & Human Potential

Rooted in humanistic psychology and verified clinical expertise, CRCT was established to bridge critical gaps in community mental health care across Visakhapatnam.

Official Centre Mission

Our Mission & Purpose

“At CRCT, we are dedicated to filling the void in mental health care and making a profound difference in the lives of children, adolescents, and adults. Our Counselling Centre aims to become a crucial component of support services extended to the entire community. Our mission is to develop healthy coping strategies and create a safe environment for everyone.”

Our Brand Tagline:

Your Life Is Precious
