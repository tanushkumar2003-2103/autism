Dedicated Autism Care

Specialized Autism Support & Therapeutic Pathways

Empowering neurodivergent children, adolescents, and families across Visakhapatnam through individualized developmental guidance, positive behavioral therapy (ABA principles), sensory accommodation, and humanistic family counselling.

Autism Support Transparency Notice: Our clinical programs are customized to support neurodevelopmental differences and nurture individual autonomy. We do not promote harmful "cures" or forced masking. Every session is grounded in evidence-informed methods, dignity, and compassionate collaboration with parents.
Early Intervention

1. Developmental Screening & Milestone Observation

Gentle play-based developmental observations to assess communication milestones, response patterns, and joint attention. Helping parents understand developmental timing without inducing unnecessary fear.

Behavioral Therapy

2. Positive Behavioral Support (ABA Principles)

Evidence-based Applied Behavior Analysis approaches utilizing positive reinforcement. We focus on practical life skills, self-soothing strategies, and functional independence while strictly respecting autistic bodily autonomy.

Sensory Living

3. Sensory Regulation & Environmental Adaptations

Evaluating hyper- and hypo-sensory responses to noise, lights, textures, and crowds. Assisting families in setting up calming sensory sanctuaries and managing sensory overload proactively.

Communication

4. Functional Communication & Assistive Tools

Supporting non-speaking, minimally speaking, and echolalic communication through visual timetables, Picture Exchange Communication (PECS), AAC tools, and social stories.

Parent Support

5. Parent Empowerment & Family Harmony

Counselling designed to alleviate caregiver exhaustion, clarify misunderstandings, guide siblings, and equip parents with confident, affectionate coping strategies in daily life.

Education

6. School Readiness & Classroom Accommodations

Collaborating with schools and educators in Andhra Pradesh to design individualized sensory and learning adjustments, exam accommodations, and predictable transitions.

Clinical Flow

How Our Autism Support Sessions Work

Every neurodivergent journey is distinct. Here is what families can expect when consulting CRCT:

01

Initial Family Consultation

A relaxed, unhurried discussion with parents to listen to developmental history, daily joys, triggers, and family observations.

02

Gentle Child Observation

Play-based interaction in a sensory-friendly space assessing sensory preferences, communication modes, motor skills, and strengths.

03

Customized Support Roadmap

A collaborative action plan featuring practical home routines, parent coaching strategies, and therapy recommendations.
